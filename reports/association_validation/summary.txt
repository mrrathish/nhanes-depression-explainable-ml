CAUTIONS
- These are cross-sectional associations, not causal effects.
- Confirm NHANES 2021-2023 cycle-specific weights and codebooks.
- MEC models are primary candidates; interview models sensitivity only.
- Survey residual df constrain Wald-test reliability.
- Curvature and overall association are different hypothesis tests.
- Different adjustment sets may have different complete-case samples.
- Curves are conditional predictions at reference covariates, not marginal.
- Income/education adjustment is not automatically a causal adjustment set.
- No automatic multiple-testing correction; interpret exploratory tests cautiously.
